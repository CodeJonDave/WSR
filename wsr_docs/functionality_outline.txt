WorkSafe Radio - Use Cases
Client Users:
- Employees
- Site Administrators
- Company Administrators

Service Users:
- Account Manager

Employee Use Cases:

1. Employee Registration & Authentication:
- Employees can register for the service.
- Employees will receive an email with a confirmation link upon registration.
- Employees must confirm registration via email before logging in.
- Employees can log in to the service.

2. Employee Profile Management:
- Employees can view and edit their profile.

3. Employees can view currently playing song:
- Employees can view the currently playing song on the site playlist.
- Employees can view the song details (e.g., artist, album, release date, genre).
- Employees can view the song progress (e.g., time elapsed, time remaining).

3. Personal Playlist Management:
- Employees can add songs to their personal playlist.
- Employees can delete songs from their personal playlist.
- Employees can view their personal playlist.
- Employees can filter their playlist by genre, artist, or song title.

4. Song Search & Selection:
- Employees can search for songs by genre, artist, or song title.
- Employees can view search results.
- Employees will be able to play 30sec previews of songs from search results.
- Employees can add songs from search results to their playlist.
- Employees can view song details (e.g., artist, album, release date, genre).

5. Flagging & Content Moderation:
- Employees can flag songs for review if they contain inappropriate content.
- Employees can view previously flagged songs (for transparency).

Song Search Flow:

1. Search Functionality:
- Search by: Genre, Artist, or Song Title.
- employees can filter search results based on preferences (such as avoiding specific terms), which will allow them to personalize their experience.
    Search Results:
        - Display song details: Song Title, Artist, Genre.
        - Option to play preview (via Spotify API) and view details (e.g., album, release date).
2. Content Check:
    Passes Content Check:
        - Display the results with options to play preview and add to personal playlist.
    Fails Content Check:
        - Providing clear reasoning when a song fails the content check can enhance transparency and help users understand the filtering process.
        - Automatically search for clean/radio-friendly versions of the song.
        - If no clean version is found, display a suggested list of radio-friendly songs based on genre and artist that pass the content check.
3. Song Preview:
- Preview: Play a 30-second preview of the song via the Spotify API.
- Song Details: Display additional song info like artist, album, release date, and genre.
4. Re-Search for Clean Versions:
- If the song fails the content check, an automatic re-search will be triggered for a clean/radio-friendly version.
- If no clean version is found, the system will suggest other radio-friendly songs based on genre and artist that pass the content filter.
5. Suggested List for Clean Versions:
- The system will suggest radio-friendly songs based on genre and artist only when the initial song fails the content check and no clean version is found.
- These suggestions will come from the songs that pass the content filter.

Flagging & Moderation Flow:

1. Flagging a Song:
- Employees can flag a song if they believe it contains inappropriate or offensive content.
- A song can be flagged once per employee.
2. Flagged Content Review:
    Site Admin Review:
        - The site admin will receive notification when a song is flagged and start the review process.
        - The flagged song will undergo an additional content check to verify whether it contains inappropriate material.
        - If the song fails the content check, the site admin can remove the song from the playlist/system.
        - If the song passes the content check, the flag will be removed, and the song will remain in the system.
3. Flagged Content Log:
    Log of Flagged Songs:
        - A comprehensive log of all flagged songs will be maintained by the system for transparency.
        - The log will include the song title, employee who flagged it, and the status (whether the song was removed or retained).
    Site Admin Access:
    - Site admins can view the flagged content log, including the status of each flagged song (removed or retained).
    - Admins can also filter the log based on the flagged status to identify potential patterns of inappropriate content.
4. Multiple Flags:
    If a song is flagged multiple times by different employees:
        - The song will undergo a more thorough review process by the site admin.
        - If the song is flagged by multiple employees, both the company admin and site admin will receive a notification for immediate review.
        - Admins can prioritize songs flagged by multiple users to expedite the review process.
5. Flagging of Already Approved Songs:
    If the song passed the additional content check previously:
        - Employees will not be able to flag that song again.
        - This helps prevent spam flagging or false flagging.
        - The system will track the status of flags to ensure songs cannot be continuously flagged by the same employee after being cleared.


Playing site playlist:
1. Site Admin Start Playlist:
- Site admins can start the site playlist, which will play for all employees.
2. The playlist will allocate songs based on:
    A. personal playlists:
        - songs will be added to the playlist based on the employees assigned to shift blocks.
        - any duplicate songs will only be stored once with a counter for each personal playlist it appears in.
        - when shift changes songs counter will be readjusted based on the outgoing and incoming employees. if the count reaches 0 the song will be removed from the playlist.
    B. pre-approved songs:
        - songs that have been pre-approved by the site admin will be added to the playlist.
        - only if the number of employee selected songs fall below a certain threshold.
3. Order of songs played:
- to ensure fairness, the system will randomize the order of songs played.
- the system will also ensure that no artist or genre is over-represented in the playlist.
4. messages:
- site-specific messages will be played after 3 songs and company-wide messages will be played after 5 songs.
- the system will ensure that the messages are played in a way that does not interrupt the flow of the playlist.
5. Site Admin Stop Playlist:
- Site admins can stop the site playlist at any time.
- The playlist will be paused and can be resumed at a later time.

messages workflow:
1. Message Creation:
- admins will be able to upload or record audio messages.
- messages can be categorized as site-specific or company-wide.
- admins can add a description or title to the message.
2. Message Approval:
- site admins will review and approve messages before they are added to the system.
- Content Check Automation: Implement an automated content-checking tool for flagged words or phrases before reaching the approval stage.
- messages will undergo a content check to ensure they meet the company's guidelines.
3. Message Scheduling:
- site-specific messages will be played after a certain number of songs, while company-wide messages will be played after a different number of songs.
4. Message Playback:
- messages will be played in between songs to ensure they do not interrupt the flow of the playlist.
- the system will ensure that messages are played at appropriate intervals and do not overlap with other messages.
5. Message Removal:
- admins can remove messages from the system if they are no longer relevant or appropriate.
- removed messages will be stored in an archive for future reference.
6. categorized messages:
- messages can be categorized based on their content (e.g., holiday, safety, HR).
- admins can filter messages based on categories to find specific messages quickly.
7. message history:
- Compliance Export Options: When exporting message history for audit purposes, allow for different formats (e.g., PDF, CSV, Excel) to suit various compliance requirements.
- the system will maintain a log of all messages, including the date, time, and content of each message.
- admins can review the message history to track the playback of messages and ensure compliance with company guidelines.
- the message history log will also include any actions taken on messages (e.g., approval, removal).
- the message history log will be accessible to site(view only for non-site) admins, company admins, and account managers.
- the message history log will be searchable by date, category, or message content.
- the message history log will be exportable for compliance and audit purposes.
- the message history log will be archived for future reference and compliance tracking.
