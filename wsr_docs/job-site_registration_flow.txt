🏢 Job Site Registration Flow (Updated March 08, 2025)
(Changes: Emphasized only site_admin is set, job_site_id is the sole link to employee flow.)

Job Site Initiates Registration
Action: Representative visits public registration page.
Outcome: Prompted to create a job site account.

Job Site Account Setup
Information Provided:
Company Name: e.g., "Acme Corp."
Job Site Name: e.g., "Acme Corp - South Plant."
Contact Email: For notifications and site_admin.
Phone Number: For verification/support.
Address: e.g., "456 Factory Lane."
Default Shift Times: e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM.
Initial Site Admin Details:
First Name, Last Name
Admin Email
Password
Action: Submits form.
Outcome: Generates job_site_id, assigns site_admin role.

Account Verification
Action: Verification email sent to Admin Email.
Outcome: Site_admin clicks link to activate.

QR Code Generation & Employee Pre-Fill
Action: Generates QR code for job_site_id
Outcome: QR downloadable/shareable; pre-fill stored for employee use.

Registration Complete
Action: Site_admin logs in.
Outcome: Sees confirmation with Company Name, Job Site Name, Default Shift Times, Employee Count, QR code.

Key Highlights: Only site_admin set; job_site_id links to employee flow, no other site info used.
